## Interpretation

1. **Plain is ceiling-bound and identical** (13.5–13.8 ROCm, ~9.0 Vulkan): the deltanet/attention
   mix does not change the bandwidth limit; for the dense 27Bs the only speed lever remains MTP
   (as with Qwen3.6).
2. **Qwen3.8 wins the MTP head-to-head**: deterministic +25/35% on ROCm and +20% on Vulkan;
   prose +8/11% on ROCm, on par on Vulkan. Qwen3.8's MTP is more profitable especially on
   predictable content (pos-2 acceptance up to 0.95).
3. **On the dense 27Bs ROCm dominates Vulkan on tg** (13.8 vs 9.0, +53%) — the opposite of the
   MoE-35B class where RADV won tg (81.6 vs 71.2). The backend choice depends on the model
   class: dense → ROCm; fp4 MoE → Vulkan fork. pp512: ROCm +11% (355 vs 320).
4. MTP speedup vs plain (Qwen3.8 ROCm): det ~2.0×, prose ~1.35×.

## Methodological note on the old numbers

The previous Qwen3.6-27B figure "MTP 32.70 tok/s / acceptance 70.7%" (2026-08-11) was measured
with n-max 4 and p_min 0.0 (different config phase) → not comparable with today's lines (rule:
comparisons only at equal phase). The plain 13.68 is instead confirmed (13.51 ± 0.40).
